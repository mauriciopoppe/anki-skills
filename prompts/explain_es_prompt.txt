Analyze the following French sentence/phrase from an Anki Cloze card:
"{Expression}"

Write a concise, high-value explanation in natural Spanish for the "Notes" field.

1. **Focus strictly on the French target inside `{{c1::...}}`** (ignore any hint text after the second `::`). Always name the target French word/expression in bold.
2. **Do NOT provide a full translation of the sentence**, and **NEVER explain Spanish dialect differences or Mexican Spanish vocabulary** — Spanish is only the language of the explanation; the subject is always French.
3. Explain **what it means in this context** AND **how it works grammatically or idiomatically** (e.g., verb + preposition, tense/mood trigger, gender/agreement, literal vs. figurative sense, register like *oral/familier/soutenu*, or a key contrast with a Spanish false friend or lookalike French expression).
4. Aim for **2–3 crisp sentences (roughly 35–75 words)**. Be concrete and useful — never write a bare one-word synonym or cryptic shorthand.
5. Output **ONLY** the HTML note content (no preamble like "Aquí está la nota:").
6. Use **HTML formatting only** (`<b>`, `<i>`, `<br>`). **Never use Markdown** (`**`, `*`, `#`, or backticks), because Anki renders HTML.

## Examples

Input: `Sur ça, tu touches un point {{c1::assez juste}}`
Output: `<b>Assez juste</b> significa «bastante acertado» o «muy pertinente» (aquí <i>juste</i> no es «justo», sino «exacto»). Con <i>toucher un point</i> se usa en conversación para reconocer que alguien dio en el clavo.`

Input: `Nous pensons {{c1::à::à + obj}} nos prochaines vacances.`
Output: `<b>Penser à</b> + sustantivo significa «pensar en» (tener algo en mente), a diferencia del español que usa <i>en</i>. No lo confundas con <b>penser de</b>, que se usa para pedir o dar una opinión (<i>Qu'est-ce que tu en penses ?</i>).`
